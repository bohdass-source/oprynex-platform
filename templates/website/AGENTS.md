Oprynex client website template. Copy this repository for each client; the client-website skill (~/.claude/skills/client-website) walks through brief, build, checks and launch.

- One file per client: src/site.config.ts (schema and comments in src/lib/config.ts). Components never hold client copy. Interface texts and URL slugs for de, en, pl, ru, uk and tr live in src/lib/ui-text.ts; a new language needs one complete block there.
- URLs: the first language in `languages` has no prefix (/leistungen), the others do (/pl/uslugi). src/routes/$.tsx resolves every path through resolvePath and answers 404 for anything else. Impressum and Datenschutz exist in German only; their copies under other languages show a note, point their canonical to the German page and are not indexed.
- SEO and GEO from the config: unique title and description per page, self canonical, hreflang, Open Graph, schema.org (business type, FAQPage, services), /sitemap.xml, /robots.txt, /llms.txt, /favicon.svg. `indexable: false` (the default until launch) makes every page noindex and robots.txt block crawlers, so trial links never get indexed.
- Legal: src/lib/legal.ts builds the Impressum (§ 5 DDG) and the privacy policy from the business data. They describe exactly what the template does: Cloudflare hosting, the contact form stored by Oprynex (processor) in Supabase Frankfurt, self-hosted fonts, no cookies, no browser storage, no tracking. A new feature (maps, analytics, booking, chat, external fonts or videos) needs its privacy section first, and anything that sets cookies or loads third parties needs a consent banner. Never invent legal data; the client confirms both texts, then legal.reviewed becomes true.
- Contact form: shared Zod schema (src/lib/lead.ts) in the browser and on the server; the server function checks the origin, the honeypot and Cloudflare's IP header, then calls public.accept_site_lead in the Oprynex Supabase with the Worker secret SITE_INTAKE_TOKEN (src/lib/intake.server.ts). The lead lands in the client's CRM workspace. Without the secret the form shows the business email instead of losing the request.
- Look: flat colours, light or dark from the config, one accent, Manrope or Geist served from the project, Phosphor icons, the landing-page-design rules (spacing scale, no background gradients, the hero heading gradient, scroll reveals, the tagline whose words light up). A test checks accent contrast.
- Commands: `bun run dev` (port 8100), `bun run test:node26`, `bunx tsc --noEmit`, `bun run build`, `bun run preflight`, `bun run cf:deploy` (preflight, build, deploy as the Worker named in the config), `bun scripts/connect-crm.ts <workspace-uuid>` (sets the intake secret without printing it and prints the SQL that registers the site).
- The preflight refuses example data (`example: true`), placeholders and an indexable site whose legal texts are not reviewed. ALLOW_EXAMPLE=1 deploys the example business as a demo.
- Never commit secrets or .env files.
